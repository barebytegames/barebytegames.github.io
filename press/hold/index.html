About the game

In HOLD, three carriers follow rails that intersect at three crossings. If two carriers arrive at the same crossing together, the run ends. Tap one carrier to freeze it. Tap another and the first immediately moves again; tap the held carrier to release it. The red warning rings help signal a coming collision. Score a point each time a carrier passes across, and decide which moving piece deserves the one brake. A four-step interactive lesson teaches the controls. The game saves your best score and settings locally.
